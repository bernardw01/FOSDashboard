Organizational value delivered

Mapped to documented feature goals

Speed & scale

Live dashboards load from an in-app Datastore, not Fibery on every cold open

Feature 036 introduced Datastore Live serving, then matured through Agreement Management typed mirrors and panel builders from tables so Pull hydrates relational rows and assembles panel JSON without Fibery labor paths. Fibery remains the system of record; ADMIN Pull and nightly jobs keep the store fresh.

Decision support

Natural-language answers on the dashboard you are already viewing

FinOps Ask (032) grounds Q&A in loaded panel data, filters, and Live vs snapshot context, with daily quotas, activity logging, Drive chat archives, markdown answers, and prompt caching so finance and ops interpret KPIs in place.

Proactive ops

Alerts reach people who are not parked on the Web App

Feature 033 delivers opt-in Hourly / Daily / Weekly HTML digests for existing Agreement and Utilization alerts, deep links back into the Hub, and an in-app notification tray for review and dismiss.

Delivery excellence

Engagement Review plus clearer project ownership and P&L drill-down

Feature 037 adds a Delivery Engagement Review route for CE / EXEC / ADMIN with calendar, Drive recordings, and questionnaires. Delivery also gained Assigned Owner filters, hydrate fixes, restored Allocated cost lines, and month-modal hours by person.

Workforce visibility

Utilization heatmap and Labor Hours stay trustworthy on Datastore labor

Feature 039 ships legend band filters, sticky headers, and Live date ranges rebuilt from the Clockify fos_labor_costs mirror. Labor Hours week picking and grants patches closed DST and permission gaps.

Executive finance & trust

Portfolio Excel, historical snapshots, and one named product identity

Portfolio Excel export (031), snapshot schema upgrade paths (009), FinOps Performance Hub rebrand, and clearer unauthorized-access troubleshooting give executives offline workbooks and operators a recognizable, supportable surface.
